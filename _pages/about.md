Hello! I'm Kaden Grace, a fourth-year Economics PhD candidate at the University of Tennessee - Knoxville. My research uses tools from applied microeconomics and causal inference to answer questions about public finance and health economics. Outside the office, I spend a lot of time underwater.

Research Fields
------
* Public Economics
* Behavioral and Experimental Economics
* Health Economics
* Applied Microeconomics

Background
------
Since 2023, he has served as Chairperson of the Haslam Doctoral Student Association. Grace earned his BS in Economics and BA in Mathematics from the University of Mississippi and his MA in Economics from the University of Tennessee.
